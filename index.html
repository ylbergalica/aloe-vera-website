How it's made

From garden to jar

Fresh aloe works best when it's used quickly, so we make our products close to where the plants grow.

Grow

Our aloe plants grow slowly over several years in sun and well-drained soil, with no chemical sprays.

Harvest

We cut only the mature outer leaves by hand, so the plant keeps growing and producing.

Fillet

Each leaf is drained, washed and opened by hand to scoop out the clear inner gel.

Make

The fresh gel is blended with oils, butters and botanicals in small, carefully labelled batches.

Why aloe vera

Nature's soothing plant

Aloe vera has been used in skincare for thousands of years. Here's why we love it.

Hydrating

The inner gel is mostly water, with natural sugars that help skin hold onto moisture.

Calming

Cooling and gentle, it's a favourite for skin that feels hot, tight or sun-stressed.

Lightweight

It absorbs quickly without feeling greasy, so it suits every skin type.
