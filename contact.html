    FAQ

    Common questions

    How do I place an order?

    Add products to your basket and press "Send order". This opens a ready-made email or WhatsApp message with your order. We'll reply to confirm stock, delivery and payment. Nothing is charged on the website.

    Do you deliver?

    Yes. We deliver locally, and local delivery is free on orders over €40. You can also collect from us. Ask us about delivery further away.

    How long do the products last?

    Because we use fresh aloe and few preservatives, our products are best used within 6 months of opening. Keep them somewhere cool and out of direct sunlight. The pure gel lasts longest in the fridge.

    Are your products suitable for sensitive skin?

    Aloe is naturally gentle and most people with sensitive skin get on well with it. As with any skincare, patch test on a small area first, and check the full ingredient list if you have allergies. Some products contain nut oils, beeswax or honey.

    Is the aloe really grown by you?

    Yes. Every leaf comes from plants in our family garden, and we harvest them by hand shortly before each batch is made.

    Can I buy an aloe plant?

    Yes. Our plants grow "pups", small baby plants, which we pot up and sell. They're easy to care for: lots of light, very little water.

    Do you make gift sets or wholesale orders?

    We do. We can make custom gift sets for birthdays, weddings or corporate gifts, and we're happy to talk to local shops about stocking our products. Send us a message.
