Where it began

It started with a few plants

My dad has always loved growing things. A few aloe vera plants turned into rows of them, and he started looking closely at what this plant could do.

He learned how to harvest the leaves without harming the plant, how to fillet out the clear inner gel, and how to blend it with natural oils and butters into something you'd want to use every day.

Friends and family were the first to try it. They kept coming back for more, and that's how Aloe & Earth began.

What we believe

Grown slowly, made honestly

We don't rush the plants and we don't rush the products. Every batch is made by hand with fresh aloe and a short list of ingredients, and we write all of them on the label.

"If you look after the plant, the plant looks after you."

We use no chemical sprays in the garden, no harsh sulphates, and no ingredients we wouldn't use ourselves.

How it's made

From garden to jar

Grow

Plants mature over several years in full sun, watered sparingly and never sprayed.

Harvest

We cut only the thick outer leaves by hand, so the plant keeps growing.

Fillet

Leaves are drained, washed and opened to scoop out the pure inner gel.

Make

The gel goes straight into small batches of creams, soaps and balms.
